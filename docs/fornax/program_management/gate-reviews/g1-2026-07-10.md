# G1 Gate Review — Engine v0 Authorization

**Review date:** 2026-07-10  
**Plan of record:** [`project-plan-v4.md`](../../project-plan-v4.md)  
**Gate:** G1 — contract and implementation-readiness review  
**Outcome:** **PROCEED**  
**Decision:** [DEC-005](../08-decision-log.md)

## Decision

Proceed with the bounded, assumption-driven Engine v0 described by plan v4.
Unavailable heterogeneous hardware does not block implementation of the engine
spine. The team may implement and validate the production contracts using the
reference backend, simulated MAX backend, deterministic multi-process loopback
transport, fault injection, and versioned hardware scenarios.

This is not approval to claim physical heterogeneous correctness, supported
platform status, or production performance. Those claims require G2/G3 evidence
from the relevant physical systems.

## Authorized scope

- Repair the planner's memory accounting and candidate-pruning correctness bugs.
- Implement the stable `StageExecutable` seam with `ReferenceStageBackend` and
  `SimulatedMaxStageBackend` first, followed by `MaxStageBackend` without changing
  the public stage contract.
- Implement the Stage ABI, framed tensor transport, credit-based backpressure,
  identity checks, failure injection, trace correlation, and deterministic replay.
- Complete a layer-group pipeline spine across at least two isolated local
  processes and prove output parity against the reference backend.
- Measure assumption sensitivity across the named scenarios in the simulation
  contract and publish results as predicted or simulated evidence only.
- Continue physical validation opportunistically as matching hardware becomes
  available; replace assumption ranges with observations rather than redesigning
  the engine boundary.

## Evidence classification

The gate reviewed **contract**, **analytical**, **simulated**, and existing
**proxy-measured** evidence. No evidence in this review establishes a new
physical heterogeneous or product claim. Reports produced under this authority
must retain their evidence-class label and assumption-set identifier.

The existing `program_governance` golden vector intentionally remains a T1
control fixture that proves a simulated process cannot silently declare G1. It
is not the live gate record; this reviewed document and DEC-005 are.

Baseline validation on 2026-07-10 passed all golden contract checks and all 257
unit tests. The local HTTP smoke cases required permission to bind ephemeral
localhost sockets. This proves the pre-Engine-v0 contract baseline remains green;
it is not physical heterogeneous evidence.

## Open assumptions and risks

- SA-001 through SA-010 remain open until replaced by recorded measurements.
- R-11: the simulation-to-hardware gap could expose unmodeled backend or fabric
  behavior.
- R-12: waiting for complete hardware would stall contract learning and engine
  delivery; the approved simulation lane mitigates this risk.
- R-13: MAX fork drift could make results irreproducible; exact base and patch
  commits must travel with every MAX-backed artifact.

The most decision-sensitive assumptions are activation bandwidth/latency,
usable device memory, backend kernel coverage, compilation stability, and
cross-backend numerical tolerance. Sensitivity results decide which hardware
measurements are acquired first.

## Exit conditions for the active sprint

Phase 0.5 is complete only when its acceptance criteria pass, including planner
regressions, backend parity, ABI negative tests, deterministic two-process
pipeline execution, fault/backpressure tests, and evidence labeling. Simulated
throughput is not an exit substitute for correctness.

## Next gate

G2 is the physical distributed-correctness gate. Its minimum packet must include
the pinned MAX build, runtime/ABI compatibility, real cross-process or cross-node
tensor transfer, output parity on the target graphlet, failure/backpressure
behavior, and a comparison of measured results against the declared assumption
set. Missing hardware may delay G2, but does not revoke this Engine v0
authorization.

## Recorded outcome

**PROCEED — bounded Engine v0 implementation is authorized under plan v4.**
Physical heterogeneous and performance claims remain unapproved until their
respective gates pass.
