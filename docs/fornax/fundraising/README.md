## Illustrative financing scenario

The existing scenario models **USD 1.5 million (approximately AED 5.51 million)
for 18 months** as a proof-and-design-partner round. It is not an approved ask or
negotiation range until XG-3 has a bottoms-up monthly cash model and principal
approval. The modeled round is intended to produce:

- a repeated physical NVIDIA-to-Apple request with numerical parity and component
  timing (G2);
- three customer workload traces, two design-partner commitments, and one paid
  pilot;
- a frontier-capacity target, measured baselines, and defensible customer TCO
  (G3 decision); and
- a repeatable pilot package operated by a fresh operator without oral context.

The financing amount is a planning input, not a valuation or fundraising claim.
Do not publish a valuation, dilution target, customer logo, principal biography,
or revenue claim until the authorized principals supply and approve it.

## Conversation posture after minimum release gates

and XG-2 resolves commercial-rights posture, the evidence supports exploratory
accelerator, grant, deep-tech pre-seed, and milestone-based proof-round

premature until G2 and design-partner evidence exist.

eligibility, application, or deadline statement must be rechecked against the
